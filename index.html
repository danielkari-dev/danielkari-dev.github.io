01 — About

From the warehouse floor to the app store.

I'm Daniel — an operations leader by day and a self-taught app developer by night. After 14 years leading large teams and building systems that make operations run smoother, I started building mobile apps to solve everyday problems.

My focus is simple: apps that are fast, private, and actually useful. No clutter, no tracking, no nonsense — just clean tools people enjoy using.

I build with Flutter, so one codebase runs everywhere. I've now built seven apps — QuickGallery, GovMate, BLIP, Blab, TideMate, Fluonix and MyGardenMate — plus a game in development, with more on the way.

Fluonix

All-format Video & Audio Player

A modern media player that plays almost any video and audio format — movies, music and audiobooks. Touch gestures for brightness, volume and seeking, folders, favourites and sorting, plus a battery-saving dim mode for audio. Its standout feature: two ways to control playback hands-free — wave your hand in front of the camera to skip, change volume or pause (like gesture control in a car), or use your voice: say a wake word and a command such as "pause" or "volume up". Perfect when your hands are busy. All offline, no accounts, no tracking.

  • Flutter
  • All formats
  • Camera gestures
  • Voice control
  • Audiobooks
  • Offline
Built · in testing

MyGardenMate

AI Plant Care & Garden Companion

Your virtual garden. Snap a photo and AI identifies the plant and how to care for it. Every plant is saved with its own name and an ongoing conversation you can return to any time — it remembers your plant's story. Weather-aware watering tips tell you when to water or when to let the rain do it, plus frost warnings. Separate garden (outdoor) and indoor plants, sorting, and care in any language. Private, on your device.

  • Flutter
  • AI plant ID
  • Weather-smart watering
  • Any language
  • Indoor & garden
Built · in testing
