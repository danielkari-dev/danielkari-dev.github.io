01 — Storybook

BOB & BEE and the Great Treasure Map.

The read-aloud adventure

A chapter storybook for ages 5-8

Bob and Bee find an old, enchanted treasure map in the attic. When they touch it together, it wakes and leads them through twenty-one extraordinary lands — from the Maya jungle, through the pyramids of Egypt and the frozen Norse Lands, all the way to the stars. In every land waits a riddle, a guardian and a lost treasure. But Bob and Bee soon discover that the most precious things aren't the golden masks or jewels — they're the friends you meet along the way, and the courage to understand before you judge. A warm, funny, read-aloud story packed with real facts about the world.
