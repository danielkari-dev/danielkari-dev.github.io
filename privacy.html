Privacy Policy

Last updated: 01/10/2026

This privacy policy applies to the mobile apps and games published by Daniel W - AppDev ("I", "me", "the developer"), including BOB & BEE: Build & Beyond, QuickGallery, and other titles under the Daniel W - AppDev developer account on Google Play. Your privacy matters. This policy explains, in plain language, what data my apps do and do not handle.

The short version

  • My apps do not collect, store, or share your personal data on any server.
  • My apps do not require you to create an account or sign in.
  • Game progress and settings are saved only on your own device.
  • I do not sell or share any data with third parties.

Information I collect

My apps and games do not collect personal information such as your name, email address, location, or contacts. Any data an app creates (for example your game progress, coins, unlocked items, achievements, statistics, and settings) is stored locally on your device using standard on-device storage and never leaves your device unless you back it up yourself.

Permissions

Some apps may ask for a device permission only to provide a feature you have chosen to use (for example, access to your photos in a gallery app). Permissions are used only for that feature, on your device, and are never used to collect or transmit your data to me or anyone else.

Children

My games are family-friendly in content, but they are not specifically directed at children under 13. I do not knowingly collect any personal information from children.

Advertising and purchases

The current version of BOB & BEE: Build & Beyond contains no advertising and no in-app purchases. If a future update introduces optional in-app purchases (for example, buying in-game gold coins) or advertising, those features will be provided through Google Play and Google's services. In that case, Google may process limited data (such as a device advertising identifier or purchase information) in line with Google's own privacy policy. This page will be updated before any such feature is released, and the app's Google Play Data Safety section will reflect the change.

Data sharing

I do not share, sell, or transfer any user data to third parties.

Changes to this policy

I may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above shows when it last changed. Significant changes will be reflected here and, where required, in the app's Google Play listing.

Contact

If you have any questions about this policy, contact me at danielw.appdev@gmail.com.
